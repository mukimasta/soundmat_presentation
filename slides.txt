12 · 08-sensor-routing
────────────────────────────────────────
SENSOR FABRICATION
Routing & connections
  1. Ring tails
     Each ring is laser-cut with a tail—conductive fabric passes through a matching hole in the board to the circuit in the cavity below.
  2. Copper tape
     Copper foil at each slice rim folds under the board—routing the signal and pinning the upper sensor stack.
  3. Board holes
     Holes in the hardwood align with every ring tail so leads reach the cavity without a via per slice.

19 · 09-electronics-arch
────────────────────────────────────────
Electronics architecture
Five modules, three kinds of wire
Power in, data up, control out, sound out — every connection is power, data, or control.
  1. Five modules, three kinds of wire
     Power in, data up, control out, sound out — every connection is power, data, or control.
  2. Power
     One USB-C cable. PD negotiates 20 V; a buck converter drops it to 5 V for the whole device.
  3. Compute
     Raspberry Pi is the brain. ESP32 is its senses and nerves — analog in, GPIO out.
  4. Sensor & LED
     224 analog zones read through two cascaded multiplexers; 108 LEDs driven via a level shifter.
  5. Audio
     The Pi streams over USB to an external DAC — clean 3.5 mm line-out to the room.

26 · 11-firmware-bento
────────────────────────────────────────
FIRMWARE
The edge bridge
  1. What it does
     ESP32 on the mat reads every zone and drives the LEDs; the Pi turns pressure into music.
  2. How it connects
     One UART cable between Pi and ESP32 — sensor frames up, LED frames down, both at once.
  3. On the wire
     Two frame types: S sends pressure, L sends colour — plain text, no handshake.

32 · 15-software-challenge
────────────────────────────────────────
Challenge
Making it run on the Raspberry Pi
  1. Problem
     Same Jam, 50 stones: Mac cruises with 5–10× headroom; the Pi pegs scsynth at 99.9% and the XRuns never stop.
  2. Solve
     Five layers of profile-guided work — frontend, jam loop, SuperCollider, polyphony, then a cProfile pass. Sound stays identical.
  3. Result
     scsynth 99.9% → 79%, jam loop 90% → 60%. Fifty stones now play stable on the Pi.

38 · 17-jam-sonification
────────────────────────────────────────
Sonification
Jam mode — how stones become music
  1. The tension
     Two pulls on every music interface — we built Ambient and Jam instead of choosing one side.
  2. Why Lo-Fi
     Warm, loop-based, forgiving — made for a round mat and strangers playing together.
  3. Harmony
     8-bar chord loop always running — live synth, not samples. You never place harmony.
  4. Drums
     Fixed play count A×1→B×1→C×2→A×1→D×2→A×1 — loops B–D add kick, snare, and hat.
  5. Beat & loop
     4/4 at ~120 BPM. One turn of the mat = two bars — angle is time on the beat grid.
  6. Melody layer
     Six rings, eight sectors — every cell is a pre-written note; scan = when you trigger.
