Power, compute, audio — under one shell

06
Power architecture
One USB-C cable. Outdoor capable.
PD trigger requests 20V from a phone charger or power bank — high voltage, low current means thin wires and minimal line loss. A buck converter steps it down to 5V close to the loads.
Compute split
Pi for sound. ESP32 for sensing.
Pi 3B has no ADC. ESP32 has 12-bit ADC, scans the 224-cell array at 20Hz, ships raw frames over UART.
Audio
"Pi's PWM audio is the ceiling on this installation's quality."
External USB DAC → 3.5mm → external speaker. Plug-and-play, no driver setup.

Top-down view. ESP32 socket, dual 16:1 MUX, level shifter, decoupling caps.
Peak load
45W
All 108 LEDs at full brightness + Pi under load. Typical ~22W at 30% LED.
Reliability
Breadboard → perfboard
Public installations can't tolerate intermittent contact. Every connection now soldered through-hole; ICs in DIP sockets for easy swap.
