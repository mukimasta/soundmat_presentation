Why Lo-Fi

Lo-Fi

Warm, loop-based, forgiving — made for a round mat and strangers playing together.

  • Warm Soft harmonics and tape grain — never harsh in a public space
  • Loop Chord loop and drums repeat — the circle is the song
  • Forgiving Pre-written notes on every ring — you stay in key

Fixed layer · always on

Harmony

8-bar chord loop under everything — you never trigger pads from the mat.

  • Progression: 2m9 → 513 → 1maj9 → 69
  • Live SuperCollider voicings, not samples
  • Change key once — melody, bass, and pads follow
